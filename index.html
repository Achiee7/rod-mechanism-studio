Help & shortcuts

Build the mechanism in Model, then select Simulation to pose it or run it. Select a part on the drawing or in the model tree to edit its properties.

Drag a selection box left to right to enclose parts, or right to left to include parts the box crosses. Hold Shift to add to the selection. Ctrl-click a second part to constrain a pair.

In Simulation, Real physics applies the installed piston force. Forced motion drives through the available stroke and flags overloads. Both modes respect the hard stops. The eye button controls force overlays.

In Analysis, choose a drive link and angle range. The graph includes angles outside the installed travel for sizing. Only reachable poses move the drawing.
